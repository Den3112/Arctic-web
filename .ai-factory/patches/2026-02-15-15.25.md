# Patch: Fix Pie Chart Label Overflow

## Context

The user reported that project names were being cut off (overflowing) in the Pie Chart labels.

## Changes

- **ReportsView.tsx**:
  - **Labels**: Switched from displaying full Project Name to **Percentage** (e.g., "45%").
  - **Layout**: Reduced `outerRadius` from 90 to 80 to provide more whitespace around the chart.
  - **Rationale**: Full names are already listed in the Legend. Displaying them on the chart caused overflow and redundancy. Percentages provide unique, quick value.

## Verification

- **Visual**: Labels should now be short numbers (e.g., "25%") and fit comfortably within the container.
- **Data**: Full context (Name + Time) is available in the Legend and Tooltip.
